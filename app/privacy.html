Privacy Policy

Healthy Habits · Last updated 28 September 2026

Healthy Habits is made by Mark Woodings. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. The short version: it stays on your device and we never see it.

What the app stores

The habits you track, your daily check-ins, mood, water, sleep, notes, monthly reflections and settings. All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your own phone, tablet or computer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and it works without an internet connection.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Backups

If you use Export backup, the app creates a file containing your data and hands it to you through your device's share or download feature. Where that file goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use Reset in the Habits tab, or delete the app, to erase everything it has stored.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Healthy Habits is a personal tracking tool and is not medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional before making major changes to your diet, exercise or sleep.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact Mark Woodings through the store listing where you got the app.
